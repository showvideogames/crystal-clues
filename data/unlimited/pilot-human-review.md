## How to mark these up

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **KEEP** | Genuinely playable. |
| **REJECT** | Should not appear in the game. |
| **LOWER** | Keep it, but rank it beneath stronger clues. |
| **REWRITE** | The connection is promising, but the clue or its explanation needs revision. |

**A blank decision is not approval.** Anything unmarked stays open and is treated as undecided.

The **AI recommendation** is the clue author's own opinion. It is not independent validation,
and it has been measured running about 13 points optimistic on this set. Disagreeing costs nothing.

## Why a clue is in this queue

Any one of: the independent reviewer called it BORDERLINE; the two reviewers disagreed; it is an
optional restoration; it was newly written in this pass; it belongs to a pair that was below the
two-good-clue minimum; it is a reference clue; the owner proposed it; one of its two sides was
hard to explain; its pair may contain two clues that are secretly the same idea; or the author is
not confident in it.
