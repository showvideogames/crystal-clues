How to use this
  1. Double-click this file to open it. Nothing to install, nothing to run, no internet needed.
  2. Read the pair panel first. Every pair section opens with Current clues for this pair — the complete canonical set, including clues that are not themselves up for review.
  3. Choose KEEP, REJECT, LOWER or REWRITE for each flagged clue.
  4. Add notes whenever something is worth saying.
  5. Export Decisions JSON when you are finished, or part-way — it picks up where you left off.
  6. Give the exported JSON back to Claude Code to apply your decisions to the dataset.

REWRITE — the connection is promising, but the clue or its explanation needs revision.

Nothing in the canonical dataset changes until you export your decisions and they are applied. Browsing this file, and clicking in it, changes no clue, no rank and no wording anywhere else. Every label above is a description or a suggestion — never an action already taken.
A blank decision is not approval. Anything you leave unmarked stays undecided and will be treated that way. Nothing is pre-filled for you: an AI: REJECT label is a suggestion, not a recorded decision, and the clue stays undecided until you click something.

Your progress is stored in this browser only. It survives refreshes and reopening the file. It is not sent anywhere.
