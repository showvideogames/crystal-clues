# x07 penguin analogy: place or cold property + a bird → PENGUIN

Owner logic (Deb West, 2026-10-19): "Antarctica + type of bird = Penguin", a location-plus-category analogy. It was later extended to cold or ice properties.

Wording rules:
- Penguins are "commonly associated" with Antarctica, ice or cold. The text never claims all penguins live there.
- The text never claims a penguin is the named bird ("a robin is a bird, and so is a penguin").
- No flightless requirement, and hints do not have to be unique.

WADDLE (rooster wattle / penguin waddle) was a parent brainstorm only. It is not selected and not recorded as a hint.

No model calls were used. Checks: bank membership, stored pairs and preferences, `rejections.json`, and the hint rule (`hintProblem`).

Left out of the ANTARCTICA family:
- BAT: not a bird.
- PHOENIX: mythical, so "a phoenix is a bird" would mislead.

Notes on the stored candidates, strongest first:
- **BIRD**: the category word itself. The most direct.
- **CHICK**: penguin chicks are a familiar sight. This is a young bird rather than a type of bird.
- **ROBIN**: a bonus double, since Batman has both a Robin and a Penguin. ROBIN in the bank also covers Christopher Robin (BEAR + ROBIN → POOH).
- **KIWI**: another flightless bird. KIWI is also a fruit, so it is mildly ambiguous.
- **TURKEY**: TURKEY is also a country. The explanation says "the bird, not the country".
- **DUCK, EAGLE, OWL, CROW, PIGEON, HAWK, HEN**: generic category links, valid under the owner's logic.

**Variety caution.** If many of these were accepted, ANTARCTICA would show PENGUIN on most of its pairs. A few strong ones (BIRD, CHICK, ROBIN, KIWI) would carry the idea without crowding.
