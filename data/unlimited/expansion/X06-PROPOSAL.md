**Honest flags for review:**
- **BLACKBERRY** is dated.
- **TWEET:** Twitter is now X, though "tweet" is still widely known.
- **PUMA** as a brand is moderately known.
- **DRIVER** for GOLF + BUS is plain.

These are the kinds of calls an independent review should make.

**Why this helps repetition:** 23 brand-new hints with 1–3 pairs each add variety without creating another 40-pair hint. That's the owner's point that we need "lots of different hints".
